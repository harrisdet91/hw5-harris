# Metric Reference

## Net Operating Income

NOI = monthly rent - monthly operating expenses

Annual NOI = monthly NOI × 12

## Cap Rate

Cap rate = annual NOI / purchase price

## DSCR

DSCR = annual NOI / annual debt service

## Cash-on-Cash Return

Cash-on-cash return = annual cash flow after debt service / initial cash invested

## Important interpretation notes

- DSCR below 1.00 means NOI does not cover debt service.
- Positive cash flow may still be thin if vacancy, repairs, or management costs are underestimated.
- A low cap rate may still be acceptable for an owner-occupant or appreciation-focused strategy, but the skill should clearly state that the deal depends less on current income.
